Transmission Media

Transmission media are communication channels used to carry data signals from one device to another. They are broadly classified into guided and unguided media.

Guided Media

Guided media transmit signals through a physical path such as copper cables or optical fiber.

Unguided Media

Unguided media transmit signals wirelessly through air or space using electromagnetic waves.
